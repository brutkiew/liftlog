LiftLog · Dashboard

Your training, on the big screen

This page reads a LiftLog backup file and turns it into a full dashboard — strength trends, muscle balance, PRs and your cut, side by side. Everything stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  1. On your phone: LiftLog → Settings → Backup — the share sheet opens.
  2. Send it to yourself (iCloud Drive, email, AirDrop — whatever's easy).
  3. Get the liftlog-backup-*.json file onto this computer.
  4. Drag it anywhere on this page, or use the button.

Strength —

Session-best estimated 1RM (Epley, capped past 10 reps — same math as the app). Warm-ups and typo-guarded outlier sets excluded.

Muscle balance

Credited hard sets per week by muscle — prime movers count 1.0, strong synergists 0.5, stabilizers 0.25 (the app's tiers). Tick marks = the 10–20 sets/wk growth zone.

Weekly training load

Volume load (lb lifted × reps, per-side lifts ×2) and cardio minutes by week.

All-time record board

Best credited e1RM ever per lift, with the set that did it. Bodyweight lifts graded on total load (bodyweight + added).

PRs 🏆

Bodyweight vs strength — the cut, in one picture

Scale weight (dots) with the smoothed trend the app uses, against your overall strength index (weekly best e1RM per lift vs its own baseline, averaged) and the pound-for-pound line — strength corrected for bodyweight. P4P climbing while the scale falls = winning the cut.
